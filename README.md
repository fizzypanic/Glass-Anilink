# Glass-Anilink
Opens a Glass Animals album in a desired streaming service

Glass Anilink is very simple in its design and code, and all it does is it:
- Asks you for a Streaming Service out of Youtube Music or Spotify
- Asks you for the album you wanna listen to
- Then loops infinitely until you close it

Other future albums are considered if and when they drop
Special versions of albums like the Bonus Levels or Real Life Edition of Dreamland are considered
Individual song choices are also considered for future additions
Remixes of individual songs may also be added

Thank you!
